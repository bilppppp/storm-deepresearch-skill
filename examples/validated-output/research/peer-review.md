# Peer Review

## Confidence

The method and evaluation claims are high confidence because they use the peer-reviewed paper and official repository. The architecture recommendation is a qualified inference.

## Weakest Link

Structural validation cannot prove that every source excerpt entails every report sentence. A semantic citation review remains necessary for real research runs.

## Bias and Over-Association

The report avoids treating the motivating article as evaluation authority and does not infer broad research superiority from one Wikipedia-like article benchmark.

## Required Revision Applied

The phrase "25 percent more organized" was replaced with the threshold, baseline, result, and absolute percentage-point interpretation reported by the paper.
